Professional Profile

Mr. Parikh, partner at Allegiance Lawyers & Solicitors, is an Advocate-on-Record at the Hon’ble Supreme Court of India with a practice of over 10 years spanning the Supreme Court, various High Courts across the country, and multiple Tribunals. He is recognised for his precise advocacy, strategic foresight, and clarity of legal reasoning.

His practice covers a broad spectrum of Constitutional and Criminal law matters, including complex issues arising under the Fugitive Economic Offenders Act, the Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA), the NDPS Act, the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, and the Companies Act, amongst others.

Special Counsel & Appointments

Being a Special Counsel for the National Commission for Protection of Child Rights, he regularly appears before courts not only in Delhi but also across several states. His approach is marked by professionalism, sound judgment, and a solution-oriented mindset, earning him the trust of both institutional and individual clients.

Notable Litigation & Contribution

Mr. Parikh has appeared in numerous significant matters before the Hon’ble Supreme Court of India and has over twenty reportable judgments to his credit. Having worked closely with many of the country's leading senior counsels, he has assisted and appeared in several landmark cases under:

  • Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, 1985 (NDPS Act)
  • Fugitive Economic Offenders Act, 2018
  • The POCSO Act
  • The Juvenile Justice Act
  • Allied legislations and constitutional principles
